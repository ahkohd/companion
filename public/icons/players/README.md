# Player icons

`roon.png` is the Roon macOS app icon, extracted from its AppIcon.icns.
It identifies the Roon integration. The Roon artwork and trademark belong to their respective owner and are not covered by Companion's MIT licence.

The browser uses this image directly. `firmware/tools/build-music-badges.mjs` derives the device badge from the same file.

`apple-music.png` is the Music macOS app icon, extracted from its AppIcon.icns. The artwork and Apple Music trademark belong to Apple and are not covered by Companion's MIT licence.
