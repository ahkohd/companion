# Companion 0.2.12

- Open Roon, Spotify or Apple Music by tapping its icon on the album artwork. Keyboard activation is also supported in the dashboard.
- Make the optional USB hardware checks more reliable.
- Link the Frimousse and Emojibase licences in the README.

App-icon taps on the physical device require [updated firmware](https://github.com/ahkohd/companion/blob/v0.2.12/firmware/README.md). Updating Companion does not flash the device. Saved settings stay in place.
